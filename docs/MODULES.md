# Product Modules

This product structure groups the 390 verified atomic records in `docs/TRACEABILITY.md` into four staff work areas, one report area, one occasional compliance area, and two background functions. The categories are product planning choices; `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` remains the source for exact obligations, codes, form fields, and accounting examples. A requirement ID appears under one module below. Compliance duties remain required even when performed less frequently.

## Money In

- **Classification:** Daily (main work area).
- **Purpose:** Capture income received by the school and keep receipts and fee records complete.
- **Main user actions:** Issue and retain receipts; record fee and other income with its source evidence; review money received through the bank.
- **Automatic actions:** Keep receipt and income-voucher numbers in order; classify and post income to the related account; carry income totals into the monthly record.

## Expenses & Payments

- **Classification:** Daily (main work area).
- **Purpose:** Handle approved spending, salary-related payments, advances, deposits, travel claims, small cash funds, and bank reconciliation.
- **Main user actions:** Prepare and approve expenditure evidence and vouchers; record supplier and scholarship payments; handle salary requests and deductions; issue and settle advances; receive and refund deposits; document travel and petty-cash activity; enter bank statement details and explain or resolve reconciliation exceptions.
- **Automatic actions:** Check budget availability and evidence completeness and assign voucher numbers. The Accounting Engine performs ledger posting, balance updates, and calculations. Staff remain responsible for approvals and actual remittances.
- **Subareas:**

  - **Expenses:** Approved expenditure, supporting documents, and payments.
  - **Payroll:** Salary requests, pay-related deductions, and remittances.
  - **Advances:** Issue, track, and settle advances.
  - **Deposits:** Receive, track, refund, and report deposits.
  - **Travel:** Travel orders, claims, reports, and vehicle logs.
  - **Petty Cash:** Small expenditure and replenishment records.
  - **Bank & Reconciliation:** User entry of bank statement details and review, explanation, and resolution of reconciliation exceptions.

## Rules that need later configuration or versioning

- Tax withholding and remittance rules (`SAM-06-037`, `SAM-11-007`–`SAM-11-010`) refer to laws, rates, and a dated value-added-tax treatment. Future implementation should keep the effective period, applicable rate, and legal basis configurable and retain the version used for each transaction.
- Retirement and gratuity contributions (`SAM-11-011`, `SAM-11-012`) include stated percentages and monthly remittance. Treat these as effective-dated rules when implemented.
- Budget-transfer limits (`SAM-06-091`–`SAM-06-093`) and report/form deadlines (`SAM-07-003`–`SAM-07-009`, applicable `SAM-10` records) should retain the rule version used for a decision or report if changed later.
- These notes do not amend the VERIFIED manual text or assert that any later law has changed. Current operational rules need separate verification before implementation.
